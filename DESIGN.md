# ElvoraOS operational design
Existing visual authority: current Elvora repository DESIGN.md and approved brand assets. Product identity: DigitalBurj Business OS supplied image lockup and iconmark.
Mode: Operate. Owner homepage exposes what is moving, what is at risk, what is earning, and what needs approval. A paper workspace sits beside a dark graphite navigation rail. Emerald guides the primary work; crimson is limited to exceptions and the New action. Tables, decision lists and one readable route overview carry density. A persistent right-hand job sheet preserves dashboard context.
Typography: self-hosted Instrument Sans body/controls, Plus Jakarta Sans headings. Regular task labels >=14px, inputs/body >=16px, secondary metadata >=12px.
Tokens: emerald #007B52; fold #025E3D; red #C32135; graphite #161D1A; pearl #F7F8F5; border #D9E2DC; muted #53675D; teal #0F7F8C; aviation #344D77; copper #8C4329; champagne #E5D1A4.
Spacing: 8/12/16/24/32px; panel radius 14px; button radius 8px. Motion: one context-preserving sheet expansion with cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1), reduced motion supported. Data uses tabular numbers, keyboard focus emerald, selection mint. Mobile retains a task-first bottom navigation and full-width sheet.
Signature: selecting any job opens the same linked commercial, operational, document, cost and activity workspace.
