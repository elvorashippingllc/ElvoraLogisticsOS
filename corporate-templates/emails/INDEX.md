# E-mail templates

Host `logo.png` on your website (or attach it inline) and change the `src` in each HTML file. Replace every bracketed field before sending.

| No. | File | Use | Subject |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | EM-01-quotation-sent (.html / .txt) | Send with the quotation PDF (ELV-014). | Quotation [ELV-Q-YYYY-NNN] – [Origin] to [Destination] |
| 2 | EM-02-quotation-follow-up (.html / .txt) | Five working days after sending a quotation with no reply. | Following up – quotation [ELV-Q-YYYY-NNN] |
| 3 | EM-03-booking-confirmation (.html / .txt) | After the carrier confirms (attach ELV-019). | Booking confirmed – [ELV-BK-YYYY-NNN] – [Origin] to [Destination] |
| 4 | EM-04-shipment-dispatched (.html / .txt) | When the vessel or flight departs. | Your shipment has departed – [Job ref.] |
| 5 | EM-05-arrival-notice (.html / .txt) | Before arrival at destination (attach ELV-044). | Arrival notice – [B/L / AWB no.] – action needed |
| 6 | EM-06-delivery-completed (.html / .txt) | After proof of delivery is received (attach ELV-047). | Delivered – [Job ref.] |
| 7 | EM-07-delay-advisory (.html / .txt) | As soon as a delay is known (see ELV-062). | Delay to your shipment – [Job ref.] |
| 8 | EM-08-documents-request (.html / .txt) | Chase missing shipping or customs documents. | Documents needed – [Job ref.] – by [DD MMM] |
| 9 | EM-09-invoice-issued (.html / .txt) | With the tax invoice PDF (ELV-023). | Invoice [Invoice no.] – [Job ref.] |
| 10 | EM-10-payment-reminder (.html / .txt) | A few days before or just after the due date. | Friendly reminder – invoice [Invoice no.] due [DD MMM YYYY] |
| 11 | EM-11-overdue-notice (.html / .txt) | More than 14 days overdue. | Overdue invoice [Invoice no.] – please arrange payment |
| 12 | EM-12-payment-received (.html / .txt) | After funds clear. | Payment received – thank you |
| 13 | EM-13-welcome-new-customer (.html / .txt) | After the account is opened. | Welcome to Elvora Shipping |
| 14 | EM-14-complaint-acknowledgement (.html / .txt) | Within one working day of a complaint. | We have received your concern – ref. [Ticket no.] |
| 15 | EM-15-customs-clearance-update (.html / .txt) | During clearance (query, duty, release). | Customs update – [Job ref.] |
| 16 | EM-16-rate-request-to-carrier (.html / .txt) | To carriers and overseas agents. | Rate and space request – [Origin] to [Destination] – [date] |
